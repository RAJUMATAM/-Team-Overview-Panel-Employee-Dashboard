# TEAM OVERVIEW PANEL
## VISUALIZATION OF EMPLOYEE INFORMATION USING POWER BI

 ## 🎯 Project Objective
Create an interactive and visually appealing dashboard that provides a quick overview of employees based on filters like name, position, department, and city to enhance workforce transparency and team visibility.

## 📁 DASHBOARD ![DASHBOARD](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8f4bb7ab-16ee-4e1a-b618-219e0e4e60c7)



## ❓ Key Questions Addressed:
- Who are the employees in a particular department?
- How many employees work in each position or city?
- What is the distribution of work types (Full-time, Intern, Contract, Remote)?
- Which team members are based in specific locations?

## 🔍 Dashboard Interaction
- Filter by Name, Position, Department, and City
- View dynamic cards for each employee with photo, position, department, contact info, work type, and location.
- Intuitive visuals that update based on slicer selections.


## 📌 KPI Ideas (Optional Additions)
- Total Employees
- Department-wise Employee Count
- City-wise Distribution
- Full-time vs Contract/Intern count

## ✅ Conclusion
The Team Overview Panel provides a clean and user-centric view of the workforce, helping HR and team leaders understand team structure, location diversity, and roles at a glance. The ability to filter and display personalized employee cards makes it ideal for internal directories, onboarding, and managerial reviews. Future enhancements could include performance metrics, tenure, or skill sets for better workforce analytics.
